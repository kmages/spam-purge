SPAM Purge Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

SPAM Purge permanently deletes the messages in your Gmail Spam and Trash folders, on a schedule you choose. It is available on the web at spam-purge.com and as an app for iPhone and Android. SPAM Purge is run by Ken Mages. This policy explains what it collects, why, and how to remove it.

What SPAM Purge can do with your Gmail

When you connect Gmail, Google asks you to grant SPAM Purge full access to your mailbox. Google requires that level of access for any app that deletes mail permanently instead of moving it to Trash. SPAM Purge uses it only to:

  • count the messages in your Spam and Trash folders,
  • read the From and Subject lines of messages in Spam, so it can apply your allowlist and show you what was purged,
  • check when each spam message arrived, so it can honor your delay setting,
  • permanently delete messages in Spam and Trash.

SPAM Purge never reads the body or attachments of any message, never reads your Inbox or other folders, and never sends, forwards, or changes mail.

What we store

  • Your Google account email, name, and profile picture, to identify your account.
  • A Google access credential (a refresh token), so purging can run while you are away. It is stored encrypted.
  • Your settings: which folders to purge, how often, any delay, and your allowlist of senders.
  • A purge log: the sender and subject of spam that was purged, kept for 30 days and then deleted.
  • Purge history: when each purge ran and how many messages it deleted.
  • Sign-in records for each browser or device you use, stored only as one-way hashes.

We do not store the contents of any email.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI or machine learning models. No person reads your data except when you ask for help, or when the law requires it.

SPAM Purge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Payments

If you subscribe in the iPhone or Android app, Apple or Google handles the payment. SPAM Purge never receives your card details. We only learn whether your subscription is active.

Where your data is kept

Your data is stored on servers we operate, and it travels only over encrypted connections. It is kept until you disconnect or delete your account.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Gmail in the app or on the website. This stops all purging, revokes SPAM Purge's Google access, and deletes the stored credential.
  • Delete your account in the app under Account. This removes your account, settings, allowlist, purge log, and history from our servers right away.
  • Revoke access from Google's side at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Or email kmages@gmail.com and we will delete everything for you.

A warning that is part of the deal

Deletion is permanent. Gmail's spam filter sometimes gets it wrong, and if a message you need lands in Spam, SPAM Purge will delete it and it cannot be recovered. The delay and allowlist settings reduce that risk but do not remove it.

Children

SPAM Purge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be shown in the app.
