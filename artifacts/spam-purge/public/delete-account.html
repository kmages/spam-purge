Delete your SPAM Purge account

SPAM Purge is run by Ken Mages.

SPAM Purge (the iPhone app, the Android app, and spam-purge.com) lets you delete your account and the data tied to it at any time. You can do it yourself, or ask us to do it for you.

How to request deletion

  • In the app: open Account, then tap Delete account.
  • On the website: sign in at spam-purge.com, open Account, and choose Delete account.
  • By email: write to kmages@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete everything for you.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes all of the following from our servers right away:

  • Your Google account email, name, and profile picture.
  • Your Google access credential (the stored refresh token).
  • Your settings: which folders to purge, how often, any delay, and your allowlist.
  • Your purge log (the sender and subject of purged spam) and your purge history.
  • Your sign-in records for each device or browser.

Account deletion also revokes SPAM Purge's access to your Gmail. We never store the contents of your email.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers after your account is deleted. Records of any subscription purchase are held by Apple or Google, not by SPAM Purge, under their own policies.

Delete just your Gmail connection

If you want to stop SPAM Purge without deleting your account, use Disconnect Gmail in the app or on the website. That revokes Google access and deletes the stored credential, while keeping your account. You can also revoke access from Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
